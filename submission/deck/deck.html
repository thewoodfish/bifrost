How it works

Lock. Attest. Lend.

1
Sepolia

Lock on the home chain

  • An originator registers the portfolio in RWAOriginVault.
  • An independent valuer prices it. The originator can't price its own collateral.
  • The owner locks it against a valuation no older than 7 days, emitting PortfolioLocked.
2
Attestcoin · USC

Attest the block

  • USC validators attest the Sepolia block containing the lock, in 8–20 minutes.
  • The prover serves a Merkle inclusion proof for the transaction.
  • Plus a continuity proof chaining the block to the attested checkpoint.
3
Creditcoin

Verify and lend

  • The pool engine hands the proof to the BlockProver precompile.
  • It decodes the lock event from the proven receipt and checks every field.
  • It opens a USDC credit line at 80% of the attested value. Draw and repay any time.
About 15 minutes from lock to funds. The collateral never leaves Sepolia.

What the proof binds

The value comes from the proof.
Never from the borrower.

encodedTransaction · proven by BlockProver
abi.encode(uint8 txType, bytes[] chunks)
Last chunk · the receipt
(uint8 status, uint64 gasUsed, logs[], logsBloom)
PortfolioLocked · emitted by our vault only
status✓ must be 1, or the lock reverted
owner✓ must be the borrower and msg.sender
dollarValue✓ sets the credit limit (× 80%)
valuationRound✓ must match the claim
Learned from the live network, not the docs

It's an ABI envelope, not receipt RLP

Wrong decoders pass their own fixtures and fail only on real proofs. Ours takes the last chunk, so it works for every transaction type.

Struct field order is load-bearing

Confirmed with differential calls: a transposed MerkleProof returns Unknown selector.

ChainInfo is snake_case

A camelCase call looks exactly like a missing precompile.

Reverted transactions are still attestable

So the engine checks receipt status before it lends.
